Put your own I Love Katamari (English) Android APK, ZIP, or extracted folder in
this directory. The filename does not matter. The tested donor is:

  MMkatamari-englishhack.apk
  package com.namcobandaigames.katamari
  native library lib/armeabi/libkatamari.so
  native library SHA-256
  86bbb9a6446c7264c6d333f91c2d5ea9cab62dc551eb581862e188460d17cef7

The port's first launch discovers the donor by its contents, validates the
native library and game-data tree, and publishes the extracted files
transactionally. Do not put the PortMaster release ZIP in this directory.
